# Quran long videos

Long-form (16:9) Quran recitation videos for two YouTube channels:

- **سَكينة يومية** – Arabic
- **Daily Serenity** – Arabic recitation with the English translation (Marmaduke Pickthall, public domain)

Every video shows the Quran text word by word, in sync with the reciter, over calm nature footage.

## What goes out

| Slot | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 – morning | A full surah from a curated rotation. On Fridays it is always Surah Al-Kahf. |
| 2 – evening | Rotates: a full juz (series: 30, 29, 28, 27, then 1 → 26) · Quran before sleep · a juz · duas from the Quran |

## Sources

- **Quran text, recitation audio and word timings:** [Quran.com](https://quran.com) API. The recitations are the classic murattal recordings of Al-Husary, Al-Minshawi, Abdul Basit and Al-Tablawi.
- **English translation:** Marmaduke Pickthall (1930, public domain), via Tanzil.
- **Backgrounds:** [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com) videos, which are free to use.
- **Fonts:** Amiri, Amiri Quran, Cormorant Garamond and Jost (SIL Open Font License).

No AI is used to generate or change any Quran text or audio.

## Run it by hand

Go to **Actions → Long videos → Run workflow**:

- To check a design, enter a `channel`, a `kind` and a `key`, and set `preview` to `60`.
- To make a real video, leave `preview` empty and set `publish` to `true`.
